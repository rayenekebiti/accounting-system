# Getting Support

We want you to succeed with Occountant. Here's how to get help efficiently.

## Help yourself first (fastest)

1. **Troubleshooting guide** — covers the common issues and their fixes.
2. **FAQ** — quick answers to common questions.
3. The relevant guide (Backup, Restore, License Activation, Update).

## Contact support

If the guides don't solve it:

1. **Generate a support bundle.** Open **Settings → Diagnostics** and create a support bundle. This
   is a single file containing diagnostics, version info, and logs — **never** your customers,
   invoices, payments, or amounts. It's what lets us help you quickly and privately.
2. **Write down what happened.** What were you doing? What did you expect? What did you see? Any
   error message (exact wording helps).
3. **Send it.** Email support with the bundle attached and your description. Include your license /
   purchase reference if your question is about licensing.

## What to expect

- We acknowledge new requests promptly during business hours.
- Issues that risk data or block your work are prioritised.
- If we need more information, we'll ask for specific steps or a fresh support bundle.

## Your privacy

Support bundles are designed to protect you: they carry no accounting data. You never have to send
us your books to get help. If a rare issue truly requires more, we'll explain exactly what's needed
and why before you send anything.

## Buying, renewing, and upgrading

For purchases, renewals, edition upgrades, or invoices from us, contact the sales/support address
on the Occountant website. Keep your license key somewhere safe — you'll need it if you move
computers.
