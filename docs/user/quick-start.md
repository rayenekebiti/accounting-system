# Quick Start — Occountant

Welcome to Occountant. This guide gets you from a fresh install to your first invoice in about ten
minutes. No accounting background needed.

## 1. Install

1. Double-click **Occountant-1.0.0-Setup.exe**.
2. Accept the prompts. Occountant installs to your Program Files and adds a Start-Menu shortcut.
3. Launch **Occountant** from the Start Menu.

The first time it opens, Occountant creates an empty, private set of books on your computer and
starts a free trial automatically. Nothing is sent anywhere — your data stays on your machine.

## 2. Set up your company

1. Open **Settings** (the gear in the sidebar) → **Company**.
2. Enter your business name, address, tax number, and email.
3. Choose your currency and date format under **General**.

These appear on your invoices and reports.

## 3. Add a customer

1. Click **Customers** in the sidebar → **New Customer**.
2. Enter at least a name (email and tax number are optional).
3. Click **Save**.

## 4. Create your first invoice

1. Click **Invoices** → **New Invoice**.
2. Choose the customer you just added.
3. Set the issue and due dates.
4. Add a line: description, quantity, unit price, and (if you charge it) a VAT %.
5. The subtotal, tax, and total update as you type.
6. Set the status to **Posted** and click **Save**.

That's it — your invoice is recorded, and Occountant automatically posts the matching entries to
your ledger. Your books stay balanced without any manual bookkeeping.

## 5. Record a payment

1. When the customer pays, open **Payments** → **New Payment**.
2. Choose the customer, date, and amount, then **Save**.
3. Occountant offers to apply the payment to the customer's open invoices — allocate the full
   amount or split it across several invoices.

## 6. Make your first backup

1. Open **Settings** → **Backup** → **Back Up Now**.
2. A restore point is saved. You can **Verify** it any time to confirm it's intact.

We recommend backing up at the end of each working day. See the **Backup Guide** for details.

## Your data, in one place

Everything you enter lives on **your computer** under your Windows user profile. Occountant works
completely offline. Uninstalling never deletes your books, and updates never touch them.
