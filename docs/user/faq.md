# Frequently Asked Questions

### Is my data sent to the internet?
No. Occountant works entirely offline. Your books stay on your computer. There is no account to
create, no cloud, and no usage tracking.

### Where is my data stored?
In your Windows user profile, under a private Occountant folder. You never edit these files by
hand — use the app. The Backup screen shows you where backups are kept.

### Do I need accounting knowledge to use it?
No. You enter invoices, payments, and expenses in plain terms; Occountant does the double-entry
bookkeeping for you and keeps the books balanced automatically.

### Will an update or uninstall delete my books?
No. Updates and uninstalls only touch the program, never your data. Your books survive both. (We
still recommend a backup before major changes — good habit.)

### Can I run it on more than one computer?
Occountant is a single-computer app per license. To move to a new computer, restore a backup there
and re-activate your license (see the Restore and License Activation guides).

### Can I run it from a USB stick?
Yes — Occountant supports a **portable mode** where all your data lives next to the program on the
stick. Ask support for the portable package if you need this.

### What languages are supported?
English, French, and Arabic. Arabic displays fully right-to-left. Switch any time with the language
button; your data is unaffected.

### Does it handle VAT / sales tax?
Yes. Set a VAT % on invoice lines, and the **Ledger → Tax** screen summarises output tax collected,
recoverable tax on purchases, and the net owed to the tax authority. VAT-exempt businesses simply
leave the rate at zero.

### Can I correct a mistake?
Yes. Occountant corrects by *adding* an entry (a reversal or void), never by silently deleting.
Your history stays complete and auditable, which is exactly what an accountant or auditor expects.

### What happens if my computer crashes mid-save?
Occountant recovers automatically on the next launch and re-verifies your books. You will not find
half-written data. See the Restore Guide.

### Is there a free trial?
Yes. A trial starts automatically on first run. When it's time to buy, activate a license under
**Settings → About**. See the License Activation Guide.

### How do I get help?
See the Troubleshooting guide first. If you're still stuck, generate a **support bundle**
(Settings → Diagnostics) and send it with your question — it contains diagnostics only, never your
customers or amounts. See the Support process guide.

### Which Windows versions are supported?
Windows 10 and Windows 11 (64-bit).
