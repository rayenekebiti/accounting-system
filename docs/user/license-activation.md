# License Activation Guide

Occountant starts with a free trial and becomes a paid product when you activate a license key.

## During the trial

- A trial starts automatically the first time you open Occountant — there's nothing to set up.
- You can use Occountant normally during the trial.
- **Settings → About** shows your current status and how long remains.

## Buying a license

1. Purchase a license for Occountant from RIO&JHK Technologies Co. (see the Support page for how to
   buy). You'll receive a **license key**.
2. Keep the key somewhere safe — you'll need it again if you move to a new computer.

## Activating

1. Open **Settings → About**.
2. Paste your license key into the activation field.
3. Click **Activate**.
4. You'll see confirmation that the license is active, along with its edition.

Activation is **offline** — Occountant validates the key on your machine. You do not need an
internet connection.

## Grace period

If a time-limited license reaches its end date, Occountant gives you a short grace period so your
work is never interrupted unexpectedly. Renew within the grace period to continue without a break.

## Editions

Your license determines the edition (for example Personal or Business). **Settings → About** shows
which edition is active. Contact support to upgrade an edition.

## Moving to a new computer

1. Install Occountant on the new computer and restore your backup (see the Restore Guide).
2. Open **Settings → About** and activate with the **same license key**.

## Troubleshooting activation

- Paste the key exactly, with no leading or trailing spaces.
- If activation is rejected, confirm you copied the whole key.
- Still stuck? Generate a support bundle (Settings → Diagnostics) and contact support with your
  purchase details.
