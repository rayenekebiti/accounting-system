# Occountant Early Access Program

## Purpose

The Early Access Program invites the first businesses to use Occountant for their real accounting and
shape it with their feedback. Occountant is a complete, tested accounting application — event-sourced,
always-balanced double-entry, crash-safe, with verified backups. "Early Access" means we are actively
improving it *with* real users, not that it is unfinished or unsafe for your books.

This document is the customer-facing description of the program: what to expect, the privacy
guarantees, how feedback works, and the reward policy.

## What you can expect

- **A fully functional product.** Invoicing, payments, expenses, VAT/tax summaries, reports, backups,
  and restore all work today. Your books are handled with the same care as any released version.
- **A welcome notice, shown sparingly.** The Early Access notice appears on your **first launch** and
  again only after a **major version update**. You can choose **Continue**, **Remind me later**, or
  **Don't show again**. It's always available later from **About → Early Access Program**. It is never
  repeated once you've acknowledged it.
- **Direct influence.** Your reports and ideas go to the people building Occountant and directly shape
  what gets fixed and improved.
- **Honest communication.** We tell you what's known, what's changing, and what we won't build. We do
  not claim the product is finished or that it has proven market fit — you're helping us learn.

## Privacy guarantees (the core promise)

- **Your data stays on your computer.** Occountant runs entirely offline. Your customers, invoices,
  payments, expenses, and company details **never leave your machine** through the software.
- **No cloud, no account, no sync.** There is nothing to sign into and nothing uploaded.
- **No telemetry.** The app does not track your usage or phone home. Ever.
- **You control what you share.** If you send us a diagnostics bundle (see below), it contains
  **only technical information** — never your accounting data — and it is sent **only because you
  chose to**.
- **Your Support ID is anonymous.** It's a random label (e.g. `OCC-3F9A-2B71`) stored on your machine.
  It is not your name and is not derived from your hardware — it just lets us line up your reports.

## Feedback process

1. **Report from inside the app:** **Settings → Support Center → Report a problem.** Choose a category
   and severity, describe what happened / what you expected / how to reproduce it.
2. **Optionally attach diagnostics:** tick "Attach diagnostics." This bundles app health only (version,
   OS, language, build channel, engine version, verification status, Support ID, redacted logs) — and
   **never** your accounting data. It's saved as a file you can send us; nothing is transmitted
   automatically.
3. **We triage and respond.** Reports are classified (correctness, usability, sales, nice-to-have, or
   declined) and prioritized — anything about a **wrong number, data safety, or a crash** comes first.
   We ship fixes only after they pass our full test battery.
4. **You can track it.** Your reports are listed under **Support Center → Your reports** with their
   status: Received → Reviewing → Confirmed → Fixed → Released.

Quote your **Support ID** when you contact us so your reports line up.

## Reward policy

We value the time you spend helping us. When your feedback leads to a real improvement, we may mark it
as **valuable** and record your eligibility for a thank-you reward — for example:

> *"Your feedback helped us improve Occountant. You are eligible for a 19% discount for 6 months."*

**How it works, honestly:**
- Rewards are **decided by a human**, not granted automatically. Marking feedback valuable creates an
  **eligibility record** — it does **not** apply a discount by itself.
- Eligibility is a courtesy, not a contract. Specific offers, amounts, and durations are confirmed by
  us directly and are subject to the pilot terms (`docs/pilot-agreement.md`).
- There is **no obligation** either way: you don't have to give feedback, and feedback doesn't obligate
  a purchase.

## Your commitments (light)

- Use Occountant for real work and tell us honestly how it goes — including what's confusing or broken.
- Keep your own backups (the app makes them; you decide where copies live).
- Understand this is Early Access: you're an early, valued user helping us get it right.

## Not part of Early Access

No cloud features, no data synchronization, no telemetry, and no changes to how your accounting is
computed. Early Access is about **learning from you**, not about changing the privacy-first,
offline-first foundation.
