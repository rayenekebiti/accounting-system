### Verify your download (optional but recommended)
Every release is published with a `SHA256SUMS` file and is digitally signed. To confirm your
download is genuine and complete, check its SHA-256 against the published value:

```
CertUtil -hashfile Occountant-1.0.0-Setup.exe SHA256
```
