## About Occountant

Occountant is a small-business accounting application built on a simple conviction: **your books
should belong to you.** Not to a cloud account, not to a subscription that holds your data hostage,
not to a server you can't see. They should live on your computer, work offline, and be provably
correct.

## Why we built it

Most small-business accounting tools ask you to trust them with your most sensitive records and pay
forever to keep access. We wanted the opposite: software you buy once, run privately, and can walk
away from with all your data intact.

So we built Occountant around a rigorous, event-sourced accounting engine — the same discipline
serious financial systems use — and wrapped it in an interface a non-accountant can operate. Every
figure traces back to something that actually happened. Corrections are recorded, never erased. The
books can't silently drift out of balance, and Occountant can prove they're consistent on demand.

## What we believe

- **Private by default.** No cloud, no account, no tracking. Even our support tooling never sees
  your data.
- **Correct, always.** Determinism and double-entry integrity aren't optional extras — they're the
  product.
- **Honest.** We publish our known limitations, verify every release against a full test suite, and
  tell you plainly when something needs your attention.
