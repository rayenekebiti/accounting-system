# Features — Copy

## Invoicing
Create invoices with multiple line items, quantities, unit prices, and per-line VAT. Totals update
live. Post an invoice and Occountant records the revenue in your ledger automatically. Track status
— Draft, Posted, Overdue, Paid — and see outstanding balances at a glance.

## Customers & suppliers
Simple, searchable lists. A customer needs only a name; add email, phone, and tax number when you
have them. See each customer's balance instantly.

## Payments & settlement
Record money received and apply it flexibly: one payment across many invoices, or many payments
against one invoice. Made a mistake? Reverse an allocation — the full settlement history is kept.

## Expenses
Log what you spend with categories and payment methods. Void an expense (in an open period) and
Occountant posts a compensating entry instead of deleting — your history stays complete.

## VAT / tax
A clear VAT summary: output tax you collected, recoverable tax on purchases, and the net owed to (or
refundable by) the tax authority. VAT-exempt? Leave the rate at zero.

## The ledger
For you and your accountant: every account and balance, the full journal, and a trial balance that
*always* balances. Reversals link to their originals in both directions. You never need to touch it
to run your business — but it's there, correct, when you want it.

## Backups & recovery
One-click backups you can **verify**. Crash-safe storage: power loss mid-save is repaired on the
next launch. If anything is ever inconsistent, Occountant stops and tells you — it never runs on
suspect data.

## Private & offline
No cloud, no account, no telemetry. Your books stay on your computer. Even support runs on a
diagnostics-only bundle that never contains your customers or amounts.

## Multilingual
English, French, and Arabic — with full right-to-left layout for Arabic. Switch any time; your data
is unaffected.

## Safe updates
Signed, verified updates that apply on restart and never touch your data. Choose a release channel
from Stable to Development.

## Trustworthy by design
An immutable event history behind every figure means your books are reconstructable and audit-ready.
Occountant governs its own data format across versions so upgrades are safe and a newer file is
never misread by an older app.
