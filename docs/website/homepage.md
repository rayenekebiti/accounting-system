## Three promises

**Correct.** Every invoice, payment, and expense posts balanced double-entry records automatically.
The books can't be accidentally unbalanced, and Occountant proves it on demand.

**Private.** No cloud, no account, no tracking. Your books never leave your computer. Support never
requires your data — diagnostics only.

**Recoverable.** One-click verified backups. Crash-safe by design: if the power cuts mid-save,
Occountant repairs itself on the next launch. Nothing half-written, ever.

## What you can do

- **Invoice customers** with line items and VAT, and track exactly what's outstanding.
- **Record payments** and split one payment across several invoices — or settle one invoice with
  several payments.
- **Log expenses** with categories and see them flow straight into your books.
- **Understand your VAT** at a glance — collected, recoverable, and net owed.
- **See the full ledger** — accounts, journal, and a trial balance that always balances.
- **Work in your language** — English, French, and Arabic (right-to-left).

## Built to be trusted

Occountant keeps an immutable history of everything that happened to your books. Corrections are
*added*, never silently erased, so your records are audit-ready and reconstructable from day one.
It's the kind of rigor you'd expect from serious accounting software, in an app a non-accountant can
run.
