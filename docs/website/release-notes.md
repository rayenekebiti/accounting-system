# Release Notes — Website Copy

## Occountant 1.0 — `[PLACEHOLDER: release date]`

**The first release of Occountant: private, offline accounting for small businesses — with books
that always balance and a complete history behind every figure.**

### What's in 1.0

- **Invoicing** with line items and per-line VAT, live totals, and status tracking (Draft, Posted,
  Overdue, Paid).
- **Customers & suppliers** — simple, searchable records with balances.
- **Payments & settlement** — apply one payment across many invoices, or many payments to one
  invoice; reverse allocations without losing history.
- **Expenses** with categories and safe voiding (compensating entries, never deletions).
- **VAT summary** — output tax, recoverable tax, and net owed at a glance.
- **Full ledger** — accounts, journal, and an always-balanced trial balance.
- **Verified backups** and **crash-safe recovery** — power loss mid-save is repaired on the next
  launch; inconsistent data is refused, never silently accepted.
- **Private & offline** — no cloud, no account, no telemetry.
- **English, French, and Arabic** — with full right-to-left layout.
- **Safe, signed updates** with Stable / RC / Beta / Development channels.

### Please note

- Occountant 1.0 is **Windows-only** (10 / 11, 64-bit) and designed for **one computer, one user**.
- It does not yet import from other tools or generate printable/emailable invoice documents — see
  our honest Known Limitations. These are on the roadmap.
- Your data is stored only on your computer and upgrades automatically. No action needed.
