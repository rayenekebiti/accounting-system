### Contact us
Still stuck? We're here.

1. Open **Settings → Diagnostics** in Occountant and create a **support bundle** — a single file
   with diagnostics only (never your customers or amounts).
2. Email us at `[PLACEHOLDER: support@…]` with the bundle and a short description of what happened.

We prioritise anything that risks your data or blocks your work.

### Buying, renewing, upgrading
For purchases, renewals, and edition upgrades, contact `[PLACEHOLDER: sales@…]`. Keep your license
key safe — you'll need it if you move to a new computer.
