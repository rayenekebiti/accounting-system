## Privacy Policy (plain-language summary)

**Occountant does not collect your data. Full stop.**

### Your accounting data
Your books — customers, suppliers, invoices, payments, expenses, and everything derived from them —
are stored **only on your computer**, under your Windows user profile (or next to the app in
portable mode). We never receive them. There is no account to create and no server they sync to.
Occountant works with no internet connection.

### Telemetry and analytics
There is **none**. Occountant does not phone home, does not track usage, and does not contain
advertising or third-party analytics.

### Updates
When you check for updates, Occountant contacts the update service only to ask "is a newer version
available?" It does not send your accounting data to do so. Update downloads are cryptographically
verified before they're accepted.

### Support bundles
If you contact support, you may choose to generate a *support bundle*. It is designed to protect
your privacy: it contains diagnostics, version information, and application logs (with any monetary
values redacted) — and **never** your customers, suppliers, invoices, payments, or amounts. You
choose whether to send it. Nothing is transmitted automatically.

### Your control
Because everything is on your computer:
- You can back up, move, or delete your data yourself at any time.
- Uninstalling Occountant leaves your books in place until you remove them.
- There is no "delete my account" request to make — we never had your data.
